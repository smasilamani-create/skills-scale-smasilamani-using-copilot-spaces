# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on acceptance criteria and feature implementation details
- **Project Managers**: Provide estimates and status updates; escalate blockers
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Partner on test strategy; discuss quality standards and acceptance criteria
- **Technical Lead**: Seek architectural guidance and design reviews
- **Stakeholders**: Present technical demonstrations and discuss feasibility

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Collaborate on roadmap and backlog prioritization; define success metrics
- **Developers**: Refine acceptance criteria; discuss feasibility and trade-offs
- **Product Lead**: Escalate strategic decisions and cross-project dependency issues
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Define test acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- **Stakeholders**: Present roadmap and gather requirements

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on prioritization and timeline expectations
- **Developers**: Track progress; manage dependencies and blockers
- **Product Lead**: Escalate risks and schedule impacts
- **Technical Lead**: Coordinate technical planning and risk mitigation
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Plan testing phases and release readiness
- **Stakeholders**: Provide regular status updates and decision requests

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure quality standards are met and acceptance criteria are validated before release. They own the testing strategy, test plan execution, and quality gate decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans aligned with project scope
- Create and execute manual and automated test cases
- Validate that acceptance criteria are met before release
- Identify and report quality issues with severity and impact assessment
- Coordinate QA resources and testing schedules
- Participate in sprint planning to define "Definition of Done"
- Conduct smoke tests and post-deployment verifications

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Ensure customer-quality outcomes and user satisfaction
- Reduce rework cycles and post-release issues
- Maintain test coverage and automation effectiveness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement discussions
- Acceptance criteria review and clarification with Product Managers
- Quality test reports and metrics shared with the team
- Release readiness assessments before go-live

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test strategy; discuss technical approach to testability
- **Product Managers**: Clarify acceptance criteria; define quality expectations
- **Project Managers**: Report on testing progress and readiness status
- **Technical Lead**: Discuss testability concerns and test automation architecture
- **Stakeholders**: Present quality metrics and release readiness recommendations

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide senior product leadership and strategic direction. They are responsible for vision alignment, prioritization authority, and making strategic trade-off decisions across projects and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve project charters and problem statements
- Resolve product trade-offs when priorities conflict
- Oversee backlog prioritization across multiple projects
- Validate that success metrics align with business goals
- Manage cross-project dependencies and stakeholder alignment
- Escalate strategic risks and business-impacting issues
- Conduct milestone reviews and measure outcome delivery

### Goals
- Ensure product strategy alignment across the organization
- Maximize business value and impact delivery
- Manage cross-project dependencies effectively
- Reduce strategic risk and misalignment

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff approvals and charter reviews
- Weekly or bi-weekly syncs with Product Managers
- Milestone reviews and post-release retrospectives
- Escalation meetings for strategic decisions
- Stakeholder alignment and priority setting sessions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Review prioritization; resolve cross-project conflicts
- **Project Managers**: Escalate strategic risks and business-impacting blockers
- **Developers**: Approve technical approach for strategic initiatives
- **Technical Lead**: Discuss architecture decisions for high-impact work
- **Stakeholders**: Align on strategy and approve scope/timeline trade-offs

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, design guidance, and risk mitigation for implementation. They ensure technical soundness, scalability, and adherence to best practices across projects.

### Responsibilities
- Assess technical feasibility of proposed features
- Provide architecture and design guidance
- Conduct technical design reviews and code reviews
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and debt
- Mentor developers and promote best practices
- Define technical standards and testing approaches
- Advise on technology and tooling decisions

### Goals
- Ensure technical soundness and long-term scalability
- Reduce technical debt and maintenance burden
- Facilitate knowledge sharing and best practices adoption
- Identify and mitigate technical risks early

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Architecture decision documents (ADDs) and design specs
- Risk register updates for technical risks
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Technical strategy and roadmap discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide architecture guidance; review technical approach
- **Product Managers**: Advise on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: Update technical risks and mitigation plans
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Discuss testability and test automation architecture
- **Product Lead**: Advise on strategic technical decisions

---

## Stakeholder / Business Owner

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Business Owners represent business interests and end-user needs in project decisions. They provide business context, approve scope changes, and validate that outcomes meet business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success criteria
- Provide user and business perspective in feature discussions
- Approve scope changes and trade-offs
- Review outcomes against success metrics
- Participate in key milestone reviews and acceptance decisions
- Communicate project progress to broader business teams
- Identify and escalate business risks and dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure business value realization and ROI
- Validate that user needs and business objectives are met
- Drive adoption and successful outcomes
- Maintain alignment between product delivery and business strategy

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and kickoff meetings
- Milestone reviews and demo sessions
- Stakeholder update briefings
- Acceptance decisions and scope approval
- Retrospectives and post-launch reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Provide business requirements and validate solutions
- **Project Managers**: Receive status updates; make scope/timeline decisions
- **Product Lead**: Escalate business risks and strategic concerns
- **Developers**: Participate in demonstrations and acceptance testing
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Validate acceptance criteria from business perspective
